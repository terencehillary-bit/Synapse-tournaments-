01 — WHO WE ARE

WE CREATE
THE ARENA.

Synapse Tournaments is a multi-sport tournament organization built to make sport more accessible, exciting and connected.

From the pitch to the court and the beach, we bring people together through well-organized competitions that celebrate skill, teamwork, discipline and community. Our focus is Dar es Salaam, with a vision to grow opportunities for sporting talent across Tanzania.

03 — UPCOMING COMPETITION

THE FIRST
WHISTLE.

Synapse Tournaments kicks off with a three-day 7-a-side football competition. Team spaces are limited, so register early.

⚽
27–29 NOVEMBER 2026 · DAR ES SALAAM

SYNAPSE FOOTBALL TOURNAMENT

Al Makhtoum Football Grounds · 7 players on the pitch per side · Maximum 12 players per team (7 starters + 5 substitutes) · 12 teams maximum.

REGISTER / ENQUIRE ↗

PLAYER REGISTRATION
TZS 30,000 per player. A place is confirmed only after payment has been made, proof of payment has been sent directly to the organizers, and the payment has been verified.

IMPORTANT
Registration payments are non-refundable once paid, including if a player or team withdraws or chooses not to participate. A maximum of 12 teams can enter.

EVENT TIMING
Daily start times, match schedule and playing rules will be announced by the organizers before the tournament.

A volleyball tournament is also in the pipeline. Details will be announced soon.
